Who I am

I build software that makes hard things simple

Resolved Logic is a one-person development studio on the Central Coast. I work with organisations (charities, event operators, industrial businesses, trade services) that have grown faster than their systems. The result is always the same: data in the wrong place, time wasted on manual work, and decisions made blind.

I come in, map the mess, and build the solution: a custom, purpose-built system that fits exactly how your organisation operates.

Working solo is a choice. Every project gets my full attention, and every line of code is written knowing someone will rely on it every single day.
